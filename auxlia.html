Auxlia

Serenity Abroad — Travel with Comfort

Overview

Auxlia is a travel support service for people who want to feel prepared and accompanied without giving up their independence. It focuses on reassurance, logistics and clear communication, so that travel feels lighter, especially when things do not go as planned.

What Auxlia offers

  • Pre-trip planning support (itineraries, timings, connections, backup options)
  • Help navigating practical details: transfers, check-ins, tickets and small admin
  • Calm, human problem-solving when something unexpected happens on the road
  • Support for solo travellers, families, small groups, or loved ones at a distance

How it works

  • A short call to understand your needs and constraints
  • A simple, realistic plan tailored to your journey
  • Auxlia stays available as a point of contact while you travel
